# ONYVEL Brand Ownership & Rights

**Public declaration date: 21 August 2026**

ONYVEL is an independently developed fashion brand and creative property.

The ONYVEL brand, including its name, visual identity, logos, original campaign concepts, original brand language, website identity, creative direction and original ONYVEL garment graphics and product concepts, is owned by **Nikolaos Lagios, Greece**.

At the date of this declaration, the commercial operation of ONYVEL is carried out through **Wisegigs Limited**, Business Registration Number **71002156**, a company in which Nikolaos Lagios is a co-owner.

**The use of Wisegigs Limited as the current commercial or operating entity does not transfer ownership of the ONYVEL brand to that company.**

Ownership of the ONYVEL brand remains with Nikolaos Lagios independently of the company, payment entity, commercial vehicle or legal entity used from time to time to operate ONYVEL, unless ownership is expressly transferred through a separate written assignment.

## Scope of this public record

This declaration records ownership and provenance relating to, among other things:

- the **ONYVEL** name and brand identity;
- ONYVEL logos and identifying marks;
- the visual and creative direction of the brand;
- original ONYVEL campaign concepts and campaign language;
- the ONYVEL website identity and presentation;
- original ONYVEL garment graphics and product concepts;
- **DROP 001** and its associated original creative material.
