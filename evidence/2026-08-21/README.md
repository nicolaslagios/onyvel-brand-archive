# ONYVEL Evidence Snapshot — 2026-08-21

This directory represents the initial ONYVEL public brand snapshot.

## Recorded facts

- The ONYVEL website was publicly accessible at `https://onyvel.com/`.
- The store presented the collection identity `DROP 001`.
- The campaign wording `REALITY WAS OVERRATED.` was publicly used.
- The wording `HUMAN OPTIONAL.` formed part of the same campaign identity.
- A first ONYVEL garment was publicly offered through the online store.
